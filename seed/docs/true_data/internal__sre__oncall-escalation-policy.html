On-call Escalation Policy

Synthetic internal document for the ADV RAG demo. Defines who is paged, when, and how incidents escalate.

Rotations

Primary and secondary on-call engineers rotate weekly (Monday 10:00 UTC handover). Each page is recorded in the oncall_logs table with the engineer, the time paged and the response time in minutes.

Escalation ladder

  1. 0 minutes: primary on-call is paged.
  2. 5 minutes without acknowledgement (P1) / 15 minutes (P2): secondary on-call is paged.
  3. 15 minutes (P1): engineering manager on duty is paged and an incident commander is assigned.
  4. 30 minutes (P1) or any SLA breach: escalate to the Director of Infrastructure and notify customer support.

SLA breaches

A P1 incident breaches SLA when its MTTR exceeds 60 minutes; a P2 breaches when MTTR exceeds 240 minutes. Every breach is reviewed in the weekly reliability meeting, and repeated breaches on the same cluster trigger a reliability review of that cluster.

Response expectations

  • Average response time target: under 10 minutes for P1, under 20 minutes for P2.
  • Engineers paged more than 15 times in a week are rotated off and the alert noise is reviewed.
  • Escalation is never a failure: escalate early when unsure.
